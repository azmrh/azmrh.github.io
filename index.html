About Me

I am a Mechanical Engineering graduate of Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology (BUET, 2026) and an M.Sc. student in Robotics and Mechatronics Engineering at the University of Dhaka. My undergraduate thesis, supervised by Dr. Sheikh Mohammad Shavik, designed and benchmarked closed-loop speed controllers for continuous-flow left ventricular assist devices (LVADs). It led to a first-author paper at IEEE BECITHCON 2026.

My master's research, supervised by Dr. Shamim Ahmed Deowan, studies how two independently mobile manipulators can fold bedsheet-scale cloth, a task beyond the reach of a fixed-base arm. I treat control theory and deep learning as practical tools for building robots that work reliably around soft, uncertain objects.

I am preparing PhD applications in robotics.

Research Areas

Robotics

  • Manipulation of deformable objects, such as large cloth
  • Bimanual and mobile-manipulator coordination
  • Soft and bio-inspired robotics
  • Simulation-first development and sim-to-real transfer

Control Theory

  • Nonlinear and adaptive control, including sliding-mode and fuzzy-adaptive control
  • Optimal control
  • Safety-critical closed-loop systems

Machine Learning

  • Learning-based control and neural controllers
  • Reinforcement learning for robot control

Vision & Future Directions

I want to build robots that handle large, soft objects in homes and care settings, and controllers that stay safe across many different conditions. I aim to contribute:

  • Coordinated mobile manipulation that extends what one fixed arm can reach and handle.
  • Adaptive controllers validated across populations of conditions, as in my LVAD work.
  • Learning methods that transfer from simulation to real hardware.
